## 2.3 Data Preprocessing – Use Cases Neumann Aluminium, GW St. Pölten and Wiener Linien

At the end of the first reporting period, the dataset of Neuman Aluminium was available, while those of GW St. Pölten and Wiener Linien were still being consolidated. In the reporting period, the datasets of all three use cases were completed and loaded into the joint pipeline.

For Neuman Aluminium, machine manuals and technical reports are processed as PDF documents containing text, tables and figures. Documents are split into chunks of about 256 tokens that keep their position in the document (chapter path and page), so that answers can refer to the exact page. Figures are extracted, described by a vision model and made searchable.

For Wiener Linien, training material, internal guidelines and regulations are processed. The structure of the legal texts is kept, so that answers can refer to the relevant paragraphs. [Anzahl Dokumente/Chunks ergänzen]

For GW St. Pölten, a dedicated preprocessing for setup data was developed. A product folder (CNC program, bill of materials, setup sheet, images) is uploaded as a ZIP file and grouped by product ID; templates, CAM working directories and temporary files are skipped, and identical CNC copies are removed by hash. A parser for the bills of materials extracts the aluminium alloy and adds it as material to every machining step. A CNC parser detects the dialect (hand-written or CAM export), splits the program into single operations, repairs character errors from the CAM post-processor and classifies operation and tool (for example drill, milling cutter, centre drill, special tool). One operation corresponds to one chunk and carries product ID, operation type, tool, diameter, spindle speed, feed, cycle and material class. [Anzahl Produkte/Operationen ergänzen] The folder upload also detects the location of the data [kurz erläutern].

## 2.4 Demonstrator development – Use Cases Neumann Aluminium, GW St. Pölten and Wiener Linien

**Neuman Aluminium.** The demonstrator supports maintenance staff in diagnosing faults. From a description of the fault, it finds the relevant sections in the machine manuals and derives the measures as numbered steps, strictly based on the documentation. Each step shows its source with page reference, and images from the manuals are shown as evidence. If the documentation does not contain the information, the system says so. The demonstrator is deployed for the company as a separate installation. [Stand der Tests / Feedback ergänzen]

**GW St. Pölten.** The demonstrator recommends tools for setup tasks, for example for drilling in a given aluminium alloy. A dedicated search over the CNC data aggregates historical operations by operation type and material and shows in how many parts a tool was used, so that functionally suitable tools can be found when a part is not available. Setup sheets and images can be consulted as additional evidence. [Stand der Tests / Feedback ergänzen]

**Wiener Linien.** The demonstrator answers questions of apprentices and operating staff on training material, guidelines and regulations with references to the relevant paragraphs. Two roles are supported, standard users and apprentices, for example for exam preparation. The demonstrator is deployed for the company as a separate installation. [Stand der Tests / Feedback ergänzen]

**Addendum for the joint demonstrator paragraph (optional):** In the reporting period, the agent workflow was made more robust (time limits, token budget, final answer on abort, inline citations, better decomposition of questions), and hybrid search with re-ranking was added. The demonstrator offers user and use-case administration with login, conversation memory, a public API with tokens and background jobs, and a skill system that allows company-specific functions (Python or JavaScript) to be added per use case. Skills run in an isolated container with controlled network access; the user is asked for permission before execution, secrets are masked, and failed calls are marked in the answer. Local language models are operated on-premise, and a tracing view shows the processing time of each step.

**Deviations or changes (Ergänzung):** The use cases Neuman Aluminium, GW St. Pölten and Wiener Linien required additional functions that were not foreseen in the original plan, in particular the CNC and bill-of-materials processing for GW, image handling for manuals, the skill system and the public API. These were implemented in the joint demonstrator and are available to all use cases. [Abweichungen zum Zeitplan bestätigen]

## 2.5 Validation and Transfer – Use Cases Neumann Aluminium, GW St. Pölten and Wiener Linien

For the three use cases, automated evaluation is combined with feedback from the users. In the demonstrator, users rate each answer; ratings are shown in an administration overview and per chat session, together with the used sources and the processing steps. [Termine und Ergebnisse der Tests bei Neuman Aluminium, GW St. Pölten und Wiener Linien ergänzen: Anzahl Nutzer, Anzahl Bewertungen, Kernaussagen]

An evaluation dashboard checks automatically whether an answer is supported by its sources. The answer is split into single claims, and each claim is compared with the cited text passages by a natural language inference model (DeBERTa, multilingual mDeBERTa); supported and unsupported claims are highlighted in the answer.

In addition, the retrieval and answer quality of the joint demonstrator was measured with the public XRAG benchmark (Amazon Science, 2025). The setup is more demanding than the reference setup: all 1,099 articles are in one shared index, and the system has to find the matching documents itself. With a locally operated 14B model (ministral-3:14b) on 100 German questions, the share of fully correct answers increased over five runs from 30 % to 35 %, the judge score from 42.5 % to 48.5 %, and wrongly refused questions decreased from 28 % to 9 %. The results are in the range of commercial models in the original study (36.5 % to 55.9 % correct), but not directly comparable because of the different setup and judge. A finding relevant for explainability: about 71 % of the citations come from distracting articles, although the answers are formally backed by sources, so "backed by a source" does not mean "correct". [Benchmark nur aufnehmen, wenn Veröffentlichung geklärt]

**Deviations or changes (Ergänzung):** In addition to the qualitative tests, an automated and repeatable measurement was added, which makes later user studies more informative. In the next period, the benchmark is repeated with more questions (at least 300), with a restricted document pool and with directly provided documents, to separate retrieval losses from model losses, and the tests with the companies are extended to larger user groups. [Weitere Schritte je Use Case ergänzen]

## 2.6 Acceptance, accountability and explainability – Use Cases Neumann Aluminium, GW St. Pölten and Wiener Linien

**Neuman Aluminium.** Repair instructions are only derived from the documentation: each step carries a source with page reference, the original manual can be opened at the correct page, and images from the manual serve as evidence. If the documentation does not cover a question, the system says so instead of guessing. The decision on a repair remains with the maintenance staff.

**GW St. Pölten.** Tool recommendations show on which historical operations and products they are based and how often a tool was used, so that setup staff can check them against their own experience. Setup sheets and images can be opened as evidence.

**Wiener Linien.** Answers refer to the relevant paragraphs of the underlying material, so that they can be checked at the source. For apprentices, a separate role is provided. [Erkenntnisse aus den Nutzertests zu Akzeptanz ergänzen]

**Addendum for the joint demonstrator paragraph (optional):** In addition to source links, the demonstrator checks before delivery whether every statement is backed by sources (answer released, rewritten or refused). Each request is logged with model, provider, processing time, citations and images; skill calls are logged and require permission where marked, and use cases are strictly separated per user. The benchmark showed that citations alone are not sufficient: a source can fit formally but not match topic or period of the question. The explainability concept will therefore be extended by a check of the fit of the cited source.

**Deviations or changes:** No significant deviations. [bestätigen]
